#Multi-Touch Marketing Attribution & ROI Dashboard

#Project Overview
This project analyzes the complete digital marketing funnel by integrating advertising spend, website analytics, and CRM conversion data. The objective is to measure campaign effectiveness, customer acquisition performance, and marketing ROI using business-oriented KPIs.
The project simulates a real-world marketing analytics workflow where data from multiple sources is combined to evaluate the customer journey from advertisement to conversion.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze marketing spend across multiple channels
- Measure campaign performance using CTR, CPC and CPM
- Evaluate customer engagement using website analytics
- Identify the highest revenue-generating channels and campaigns
- Calculate marketing KPIs including ROAS and CAC
- Build an integrated Marketing Performance Dashboard

# Key Business Insights
- Google Search generated the highest conversion revenue under the last-touch attribution model.
- Campaign performance varied significantly across CTR, CPC and revenue.
- High-performing campaigns delivered greater impressions per advertising dollar.
- Customer engagement differed across marketing channels.
- Premium products generated a larger share of revenue.
- Revenue varied across countries, indicating regional growth opportunities.
- Integrating advertising, website, and CRM data enabled a complete view of marketing performance.
